# Using Artificial Intelligence to work with Legal Documents

Agentic AI tools allow you to analyze, summarize, modify, and otherwise work with documents using a natural language, such as English.

Legal Agent takes this a step further when working with legal documents, such as contracts and service agreements. Legal Agent understands the language and structure of legal documents, allowing you to ask questions and prompt it with information relevant to the legal industry.

In this sesssion, you will learn how to use Legal Agent to analyze and modify a document and to build reusable skills based on your organization's playbook.
